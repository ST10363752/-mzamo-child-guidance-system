About Mzamo

Mzamo Child Guidance & Training Initiative of KZN was established in January 1981 in response to the critical need for services for vulnerable children with disabilities in Black communities.

We use an inter-disciplinary approach to ensure each child is properly assessed and supported for their maximum development.

  • Holistic Child Development
  • Multi-Disciplinary Team
  • Family Empowerment
  • Community Support

Our Services

We provide comprehensive, professional services to support children with disabilities and their families.

🤝

Welfare Services

Psychological, social, physical, and cognitive development support for children with disabilities.

👩‍⚕️

Professional Assessments

Multi-disciplinary evaluations by Social Workers, Paediatricians, Psychologists, and Therapists.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent & Caregiver Training

Workshops on caring for children with cerebral palsy, home stimulation, and ongoing support.

🧠

Therapeutic Groups

Support for children with learning barriers and low self-esteem through group facilitation.

📢

Awareness Programmes

Community education on social issues and the rights of children with disabilities.

🏠

Family Empowerment

Skills training, support groups, and ongoing assistance for families of children with disabilities.
