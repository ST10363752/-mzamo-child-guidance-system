Our Services

Comprehensive, professional services for children with disabilities and their families.

🤝

Welfare Services

Psychological, social, physical, and cognitive development support for children with disabilities and their families.

👩‍⚕️

Professional Assessments

Multi-disciplinary evaluations by Social Workers, Paediatricians, Educational Psychologists, Speech, Occupational, and Physiotherapists.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent & Caregiver Training

Workshops on caring for children with cerebral palsy, home stimulation techniques, and ongoing professional support.

🧠

Therapeutic Groups

Support groups for children with learning barriers and low self-esteem, facilitated by qualified professionals.

📢

Awareness Programmes

Community education on social issues and the rights of children as part of human rights advocacy.

🏠

Family Empowerment

Skills training, group facilitation, and support groups for parents, caregivers, and families.

🔄

Referral Services

Proper assessment and placement of children for their maximum development and gain.

🤗

Ongoing Support

Continuous professional services and support for families of children with disabilities.
